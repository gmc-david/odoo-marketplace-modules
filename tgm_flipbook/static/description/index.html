How it works

  1. Install — the Flipbooks menu appears under Website Configuration.
  2. Create a flipbook — enter a title and URL slug (e.g. spring-2026).
  3. Upload your PDF — click Upload PDF, drag and drop, watch the progress bar.
  4. Publish — toggle Published on Website, and your flipbook is live at /flipbook/your-slug.
Pro tip: Link the public flipbook URL from your website menu, product pages, or email campaigns — and watch engagement jump.

FAQ

How big a PDF can I upload?

Up to 2 GiB. Uploads stream in chunks to avoid browser and server memory spikes, with a live progress bar during upload.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. On phones the viewer automatically switches to single-page portrait mode with touch/swipe navigation. On tablets and desktops you get the two-page spread with realistic flip animation.

Will my flipbook be indexed by Google?

Yes. Each published flipbook is served from your own domain at a clean URL (/flipbook/your-slug), is included in the Odoo website sitemap, and carries the flipbook title and description as proper HTML.

How many flipbooks can I publish?

As many as you like. No per-publication limits, no per-view limits, no bandwidth caps — it's your Odoo server.

Do pages look crisp when zoomed or on retina screens?

Yes. Pages are re-rendered from the PDF's vector data on demand at device-pixel-ratio resolution, so they stay sharp on 4K monitors, retina laptops, and high-DPI phones.

Does it replace my existing catalogue or product pages?

No. Flipbooks sit alongside your existing website — you can link to them from your menu, product pages, homepage, blog, or email campaigns. Perfect complement to your existing eCommerce catalogue.

Can I unpublish a flipbook without deleting it?

Yes — each flipbook has its own Published on Website toggle. Toggle off and the public URL returns 404 until you're ready to re-launch.

Does it work on Odoo Community and Enterprise?

Yes, both. The only dependency is the standard website module.
